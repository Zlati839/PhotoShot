# PhotoShot
A puzzle gmame about taking pictures and pasting them around the map to make your way to the finish flag

## Special tiles
There are special tiles around the map designed to make it harder for the player to reach the end like
FirePlaces - Pictures cant be placed over them and cant be walked over 
Void - blocks taking pictures if they are inside the 3x3 grid and cant be walked over
Red flag - the finish to a level and cant be copied
Gray flag - the finish to a level and CAN be copied 

## Levels 
For now there a red 3 levels the 1st one being something like a tutorial level 
2nd level a wall of fires is blocking the way
3rd level a straight path with a pit fall on both sides and the finish is blocked
